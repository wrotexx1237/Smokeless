The CBT Integration

Our methodology is rooted in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT). By identifying the emotional and environmental triggers associated with smoking, the app helps users rewrite their neural responses to cravings.

  • Mindfulness-Based Intervention: Real-time prompts to pause and reflect on craving intensity.
  • Neural Mapping: Tracking behavioral success to reinforce positive feedback loops in the brain.

Metabolic Tapering Algorithm

Developed in collaboration with health experts, our v4.2 algorithm calculates a reduction pace that keeps your withdrawal symptoms at a manageable level. This significantly increases the success rate compared to "Cold Turkey" methods.
