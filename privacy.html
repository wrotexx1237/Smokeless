Sovereignty.

This Privacy Protocol outlines our commitment to your behavioral data sovereignty. We do not "collect" data; we "sync" it to your private clinical vault.

1. Neural Marker Consent

By using the Engine v4.2, you authorize the local encryption of heart-rate variability and stress markers. This data never leaves your device in a readable state. It is hashed and siloed against your private key.

2. Zero-Third-Party Policy

We believe that health data is a human right. As such, SmokeLess Pro has a structural technical barrier against third-party access. We have no "Data Sharing" toggle because the infrastructure for it does not exist in our codebase.

3. Right to Final Exit

You maintain a "Master Killswitch." Upon activation, all behavioral mappings, clinical trajectories, and identity markers are purged from all synchronization clusters within 120 seconds.
