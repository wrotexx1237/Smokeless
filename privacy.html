Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

1. Introduction

SmokeLess Pro ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as information automatically collected during your use of the app:

  • Personal Habits: Smoking frequency, goals, and reduction progress.
  • Usage Data: Features accessed, time spent, and interactions.
  • Device Information: Model, OS version, and unique identifiers.
  • Financial Data: Transactions processed via Stripe (we do not store card details).

3. Use of Data

Your data is used to provide a personalized experience, including:

  • Creating custom tapering schedules.
  • Calculating health milestones and financial savings.
  • Improving app performance and features.
  • Sending critical push notifications related to your plan.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to enhance SmokeLess Pro:

  • Stripe: Payment processing.
  • Google AdMob: Personalized advertisements.
  • Firebase: Push notifications and analytics.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy laws, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can perform most of these actions directly within the app settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: support@smokeless.xk
